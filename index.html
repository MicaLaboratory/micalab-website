mica lab

music interaction and computational arts · UWE Bristol

The music interaction and computational arts (mica) lab is an inclusive, values-led research group that develops and evaluates novel technologies for creative expression. The lab is located in The Bridge Studios: a cutting-edge creative technology facility funded by the Arts and Humanities Research Council (AHRC), the West of England Mayoral Combined Authority (WEMCA) and UWE, Bristol.

Outside-In

About the project

Outside Interactions is a UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship exploring new ways to co-design digital musical instruments with disabled musicians.
